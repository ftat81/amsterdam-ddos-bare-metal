## Why "Unmetered" Actually Matters in Europe

Let's get the obvious stuff out of the way first. A **metered** plan bills you for the data you transfer — say, 30 TB included, then $X per extra terabyte. An **unmetered** plan flips that model: you get a fixed port speed (1 Gbps, 10 Gbps, sometimes higher), and the provider stops counting terabytes. The port speed becomes your only ceiling.

Here's the part that doesn't get said enough: a 1 Gbps unmetered port, if you pinned it 24/7 for a month, moves roughly **330 TB** of data. That's not a theoretical number — it's the actual ceiling. Most workloads never get near it, but the point is the bill doesn't change whether you use 2 TB or 200 TB.

For European deployments specifically, this matters more than people realize. European audiences expect low latency, GDPR-aligned infrastructure, and stable routing through dense peering hubs like **AMS-IX** in Amsterdam. When you combine unmetered bandwidth with a data center sitting on top of one of Europe's biggest internet exchanges, you get a setup that handles media streaming, game servers, VPN endpoints, high-traffic eCommerce, and CDN edge nodes without the monthly invoice anxiety.
